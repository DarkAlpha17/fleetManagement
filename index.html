Fuel events — theft · rapid drop · refuel

        
A drop of ≥5% (or ≥10 L) against the smoothed baseline raises an event.
        With ignition off / engine stopped it is flagged as suspected theft; while running it logs as a rapid drop (possible leak or heavy draw).
        A matching rise logs as a refuel.
